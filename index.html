Features

Everything your classroom needs.
In one app.

Video Lectures

Stream from YouTube or Google Drive with screenshot protection, rotation support, and a distraction-free player.

Offline PDF Notes

PDFs auto-download in the background. Open them offline anytime — no internet needed for studying.

Smart Quizzes

Difficulty-weighted question banks, timed exams, practice mode, main & makeup quizzes, and instant grading.

Real-Time Chat

Message your instructor directly. Course-specific channels, voice messages, file attachments, and read receipts.

Push Notifications

Never miss an announcement, new lecture, quiz deadline, or grade update. Instant alerts to your device.

Rich Announcements

Instructors post announcements with images, PDFs, and exam details — pinned to the top of each course so nothing gets missed.

Published Grades

Quiz results reach students the moment you release them — score, percentage, attempts, and a correct/wrong breakdown, all in-app.

Multi-Course

One account, every course. Students switch between enrolled courses instantly; instructors manage them all from a single dashboard.

Dark Mode

Study at night without eye strain. Full dark theme across every page — toggle anytime from settings.
